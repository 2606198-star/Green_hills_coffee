About Our Plantation

Green Hills Coffee Plantation welcomes visitors, tourists and potential coffee buyers to learn about coffee farming. Our plantation grows coffee and explains the work involved in caring for coffee plants, harvesting berries and preparing beans for the market.

Learn About Coffee

Coffee Varieties Grown

The four coffee varieties grown at the plantation are:

  • Arabica
  • Robusta
  • Batian
  • Ruiru 11

Coffee Production Process

The five main stages, from planting to packaging, are:

  1. Planting coffee seedlings.
  2. Caring for and maintaining the coffee plants.
  3. Harvesting ripe coffee berries.
  4. Processing and drying the coffee beans.
  5. Roasting and packaging the coffee.

Coffee Plantation Activities

Pruning
Removing unwanted branches to help the plant grow well.
Harvesting
Picking ripe coffee berries from the coffee plants.
Drying
Removing moisture from processed coffee beans before storage.
